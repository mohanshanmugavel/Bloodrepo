About Lifepluse

Lifepluse is a community-driven blood donation platform connecting voluntary donors directly with patients, doctors, and hospitals.

Direct Donor Connection: Reach nearby available donors directly with contact details and requests.
Real-Time Request Tracker: Track when a donor accepts and stays in touch until donation is fulfilled.
Free & Volunteer-Driven: Zero intermediary charges, completely transparent and patient-focused.

Our Mission

To eliminate blood shortage delays by establishing a reliable and organized donor network across every district.

Our Vision

A future where access to lifesaving blood is quick, transparent, and universally accessible.
